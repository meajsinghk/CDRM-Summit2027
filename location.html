CDRM Summit 2027

Location

Luxor Hotel | 3900 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119

The Summit venue this year is the Luxor Hotel, right on the southern end of the Las Vegas Strip. Sessions will take place in the Lotus Ballroom on the first floor.

Amenities include complimentary access to the Oasis Pool complex and 24-hour fitness center, plus convenient access to the on-site Nurture Spa, rotating exhibitions like Titanic: The Artifact Exhibition and Bodies: The Exhibition, and a variety of bars and restaurants.

The venue is within easy walking distance of Mandalay Bay, where you can shop or visit the Shark Reef Aquarium, and the MGM Grand. Stay on-site for maximum ease or venture out to soak up the energy and explore.

Arriving in Las Vegas

Getting to the Venue

The Luxor is conveniently close to both major airports. Travel information from both points of arrival is below.

Harry Reid International Airport (LAS)

  • Rideshare: Uber or Lyft typically takes 10-15 minutes. Pricing is usually $20-30 off-peak and $25-40+ during peak or surge hours.
  • Taxi: About 10-15 minutes; fares are usually $21-25, plus tip.
  • Public bus (RTC): About 30-35 minutes with one transfer; approximately $6-8 total for a single ride. Take Route 109 to South Strip Transit Terminal, then the Deuce northbound to the Luxor/Mandalay Bay stop. See the RTC trip planner and fares.

North Las Vegas Airport (VGT)

  • Rideshare or taxi: About 20-25 minutes, depending on traffic. Prices range from $30-60 during peak or surge hours.
  • Car rental: Reservations in advance are best. Enterprise, Hertz, Avis, and Budget are located at the airport.
Stay at the Venue

Hotel Block

We encourage attendees to stay on-site to take advantage of our competitive room rate and help us fulfill contractual commitments. Staying at the venue is a great opportunity to mingle with fellow attendees after hours. Plus, you will be steps from the Summit sessions, which means a later wake-up call. No rideshares or long walks required; you can simply focus on learning and networking.

Book early to secure your spot and discounted rate. Availability in the Summit hotel block is limited, and rooms will fill up as we approach the event date.
